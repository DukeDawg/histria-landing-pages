How the Princess Met the Dragon
For ages 5 to 9. By Tatiana Speranskaia, illustrated by Yurii Speranskii.
A brave young princess doesn't find her best friend among the children of her kingdom. She finds him in a forest by the river: a huge, fire-breathing dragon covered in green scales. Their friendship gets tested, the way real friendships do, and it holds.
