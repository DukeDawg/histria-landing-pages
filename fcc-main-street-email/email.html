Welcome to Gopher Prairie

Carol Kennicott, a bright young idealist from the city, marries a country doctor and arrives in Gopher Prairie, Minnesota, expecting a cozy village ripe for culture and reform. She finds a fortress of smugness, suspicion and conformity, where the greatest sin is daring to be different.

So she declares a quiet war. She tries art, literature and civic improvement, and the town fights back with the weapons it knows best: polite indifference and gossip. As her marriage to the practical Dr. Kennicott strains, Carol has to choose between crushing her own spirit and being cast out of the only world she has.

Before F. Scott Fitzgerald took on the coasts, Lewis took on the middle of the country. A century later, the town behind the respectable façades still looks familiar.

Sinclair Lewis

Lewis was born in 1885 in Sauk Centre, Minnesota, and educated at Yale. Main Street made his name, and he followed it with Babbitt, Arrowsmith and Elmer Gantry. In 1930 he became the first American to win the Nobel Prize in Literature.
