The Story Behind the Phrase

What did “It” mean in 1927?

Novelist Elinor Glyn used It for a quality more mysterious than beauty, money, or status: a natural power that made someone compelling before they had even spoken.

The book brought the idea to readers. The film made Clara Bow its face. This free kit puts the novel, the star, and the history back together.
